At dusk the cliff‑torn shoreline glows with the last amber slivers of daylight, the jagged rocks catching the sky’s bruised purples and pinks as the sea sighs against them. A lone lighthouse crowns the headland, its slate tower silhouetted against the fading horizon, a thin amber beam already flickering to life and sweeping across the restless waves. The wind carries the salty tang of spray, and shadows lengthen, draping the craggy outcrops in a soft, bluish hush that anticipates the night’s calm glow.
