At dusk the cliffs crumble into jagged teeth of stone, their dark silhouettes softened by the last blush of amber sky. Waves crash relentless against the weather‑worn rocks, sending salty spray into the cooling air, while the tide curls into frothy white ribbons that vanish into shadowy coves. perched defiantly on the headland, the lighthouse stands like a solitary sentinel, its lantern glimmering a soft, amber glow that flickers against the deepening twilight, guiding any wayward vessel through the encroaching night.
