The sun sinks behind a jagged stretch of rock, bathing the headland in a muted amber glow. A solitary white lighthouse stands sentinel, its lantern faintly flickering against low, brooding grey clouds that cling to the horizon. Below, the sea lies smooth and glassy, reflecting the dwindling light in quiet, endless ripples. A gentle breeze stirs the salty air, carrying the faint scent of seaweed.
