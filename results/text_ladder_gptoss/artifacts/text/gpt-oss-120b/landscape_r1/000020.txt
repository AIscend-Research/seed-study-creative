The sun slipped low over the rolling horizon, casting a golden veil across a wide, open valley. Far in the distance, jagged peaks—still capped with the last stubborn patches of snow—silhouetted against a sky that shifted from amber to deepening violet. Below the mountains, a river wound its way through the grasslands like a shimmering ribbon, its surface catching the waning light and flickering with every gentle ripple.

The meadow that stretched on either side of the water was a patchwork of wildflowers: bright orange poppies, soft lavender lupines, and delicate white daisies that swayed in the evening breeze. Tall grasses whispered together, their rustling a soft chorus that blended with the distant call of a lone hawk circling overhead.

To the left, a copse of ancient oak trees stood, their knotted trunks thick with age and their leaves a rich emerald that glowed in the fading sun. A narrow, winding trail snaked through the woods, disappearing into a thicket of birch and pine where the scent of pine resin rose faintly on the air.

At the valley floor, a small wooden bridge spanned the river, its weathered planks slick with the day’s last dew. Beyond it, a cottage with a stone chimney rested against a gentle rise, smoke curling upward in thin, lazy spirals. Warm light seeped from its windows, hinting at the comfort inside.

As twilight deepened, the sky blushed into shades of indigo and indigo‑blue, and the first stars began to prick the heavens. Fireflies blinked to life among the reeds, their tiny lanterns dancing over the water’s surface, while a gentle mist began to rise from the river, wrapping the landscape in a soft, dream‑like hush.

In that moment, the valley seemed to hold its breath—a perfect blend of rugged mountain majesty, tranquil water, and the quiet, living pulse of the land beneath the ever‑watchful sky.
