As the sun slipped behind the horizon, the rugged coastline was washed in a bruised‑purple glow, the jagged rocks catching the last amber slivers of light before surrendering to the deepening twilight. Waves crashed against the craggy headland, sending frothy spray that glistened like shattered glass in the fading light. Perched atop the highest outcrop, the lighthouse stood resolute—its white lantern tower tinged with violet, its red lantern room a solitary beacon poised to sweep its steady, warm beam across the darkening sea, guiding ships through the night‑kissed shrouds of mist that began to rise from the water.
