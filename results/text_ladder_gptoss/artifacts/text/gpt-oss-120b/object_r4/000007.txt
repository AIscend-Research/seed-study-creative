A solitary blue ceramic teapot rests perfectly centered on a plain white tabletop, its glossy glaze catching the light in subtle swirls of cobalt and sky‑blue. The rounded body, smooth and squat, tapers into a curved spout that curls delicately toward the front, while the gently flared lid, crowned with a tiny amber knob, hints at the warmth within. The stark contrast between the vibrant, cool hue of the teapot and the crisp, neutral expanse of the table creates a quiet, balanced composition, inviting the eye to linger on the simple elegance of the everyday object.
