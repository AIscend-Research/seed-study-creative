At dusk the cliffs turn to bruised violet, their jagged edges softened by the last amber glow slipping across the sea. Waves crash against the weather‑worn rocks, sending spray that catches the fading light like fleeting diamonds. Perched on the headland, the lighthouse stands stoic, its white tower outlined against the deepening sky, its red lantern flashing a steady, warm pulse that guides travelers home as the horizon swallows the sun.
