The sun sinks low over the restless sea, casting a bruised‑purple glow that stains the craggy cliffs in shades of slate and amber. Waves crash against the jagged rocks, spraying salty mist that catches the fading light like scattered diamonds. At the very tip of the headland, the lighthouse stands resolute—a stout stone tower with a weather‑worn lantern that flickers to life, its beam sweeping across the darkening water, a steadfast beacon guiding mariners through the twilight hush.
