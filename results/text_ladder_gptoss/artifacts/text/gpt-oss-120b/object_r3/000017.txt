The blossoms seem to float just above the blue, lending an air of quiet elegance. Below the lilies, a subtle speckling of tiny silver flecks catches the light, adding a whisper of sparkle that suggests the teapot was meant for special occasions.

The table itself is a warm, light‑stained oak, its grain visible in gentle, linear patterns that echo the teapot’s own organic curves. A thin, ivory‑colored linen napkin lies casually folded to the side, its edge catching a sliver of the teapot’s blue reflection. Near the teapot’s base, a small ceramic saucer—matching in hue but with a slightly deeper, almost indigo tone—holds a neatly placed tea bag string, its tag fluttering like a tiny flag.
