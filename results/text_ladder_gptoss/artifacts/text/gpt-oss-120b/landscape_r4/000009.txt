At dusk the headland bruises the sky with deep indigo and the last gold of the sun slips behind jagged cliffs, casting long, amber‑colored shadows across the weather‑worn rocks. The sea gurgles against tide‑pools and sprays a thin, silver mist that catches the fading light. Perched atop the tallest outcrop, the lighthouse stands like a steadfast sentinel, its lantern glowing a warm, amber eye that sweeps a slow, steady beam over the restless waves, guiding any wandering vessel home through the growing twilight.
