At dusk, the cliffs tumble in jagged layers of dark stone, their edges softened by the lingering pink‑orange glow of the setting sun. Waves crash against the weather‑worn rocks, sending salty spray into the cool air, while gulls wheel in thin silhouettes above. Perched on the headland, the lighthouse stands like a steadfast sentinel, its white tower catching the last pinch of light before its lantern flickers to life, casting a steady amber beam that sweeps across the rolling sea and the shadows of the craggy shore.
