As the sun slips below the horizon, the rugged headland is bathed in a soft, amber‑purple glow, the cliffs catching the last light like weathered stone statues. Waves crash against the jagged rocks, sending frothy spray into the cool evening air, while the sky deepens into violet shades streaked with fading gold. Perched at the very edge, the lighthouse stands steadfast—a stark, white tower crowned with a brass lantern that begins to pulse its rhythmic beam, cutting through the gathering dusk and guiding sailors home across the restless sea.
