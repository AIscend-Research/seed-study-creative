As the sun sinks behind the restless sea, the headland is painted in bruised purples and molten gold, its jagged cliffs catching the last flickers of daylight. Waves crash against weather‑worn stones, sending spray that glistens like tiny diamonds in the fading light. Perched atop the highest outcrop, the lighthouse stands like a solemn sentinel, its white tower haloed by a soft amber glow that spills from the lantern room, guiding weary mariners home through the encroaching twilight.
