As twilight bleeds into the sky, the rugged headland is silhouetted against a wash of muted purples and bruised oranges. Jagged cliffs tumble into restless waves that slap the shore with a steady, salty hiss, while scattered sea‑foam patches glisten like silver soot. Crowning the highest crag, the lighthouse stands like a steadfast sentinel, its weather‑worn stone walls catching the last amber glow of the setting sun, and its lantern beginning to pulse a warm, amber beacon that steadies the darkness beyond the rolling surf.
