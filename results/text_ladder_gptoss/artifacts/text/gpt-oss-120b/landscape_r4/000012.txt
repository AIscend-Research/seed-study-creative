As twilight spreads its violet hue across the sky, the rugged coastline shimmers with the last amber glints of fading sunlight. Jagged cliffs rise like stone sentinels, their weather‑worn faces dotted with sea‑sprayed vegetation that clings stubbornly to the sheer drops. At the tip of the headland, a solitary lighthouse stands steadfast, its white tower and red lantern cone catching the dusky glow, while a thin beam begins to sweep across the rolling surf, guiding the day’s final whispers of waves toward the distant horizon.
