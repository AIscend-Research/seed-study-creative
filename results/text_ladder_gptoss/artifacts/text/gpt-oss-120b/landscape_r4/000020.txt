As dusk settles over the shore, the sky bruises into deep purples and amber streaks that melt into the churning gray of the sea. Jagged rocks jut from the water like broken teeth, their surfaces slick with sea‑foam that catches the fading light. At the tip of the headland, a steadfast lighthouse stands, its white tower silhouetted against the twilight, a lone, amber beam beginning to pulse rhythmically across the rolling waves, guiding any wayward vessels home through the gathering night.
