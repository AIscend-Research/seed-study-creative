As the sun slips behind the jagged cliffs, the sea catches the last amber glow and turns a molten gold that shimmers across the tide‑pools and slick stones. Dark silhouettes of craggy rock jut out like ancient sentinels, their edges softened by the dawning mist that rolls in from the water. At the very tip of the headland, the lighthouse stands tall and steadfast, its white façade brushed with the fading light, while its lantern glows a warm, amber pulse that sweeps over the restless waves—guiding ships home as the horizon deepens into bruised purples and indigo.
