At dusk the headland is a jagged silhouette against the bruised sky, the sea clinging to the darkening cliffs in frothy, restless rushes. Wet stones glisten with the last amber glow of the setting sun, while gulls wheel low over the crashing surf. At the edge, the lighthouse stands like a stoic sentinel, its white tower paling to a soft lavender hue as the lantern’s amber eye awakens, casting a steady, hopeful beam that sweeps over the churning waters, guiding the tide‑lit night home.
