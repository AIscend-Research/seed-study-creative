The sky blushed amber and violet as the sun slipped behind the jagged cliffs, casting long shadows across the craggy shore. Waves crashed against the rough basalt outcrops, sending up a fine mist that caught the fading light in fleeting silver threads. At the very tip of the headland, a solitary lighthouse stood vigil, its white tower silhouetted against the twilight horizon and its lantern beginning to pulse a warm amber glow that reflected off the restless sea, guiding distant ships home through the gathering night.
