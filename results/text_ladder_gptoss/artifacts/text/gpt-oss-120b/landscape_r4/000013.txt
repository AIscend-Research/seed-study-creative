At dusk, the headland erupts in a rugged silhouette of jagged rocks, their edges softened by the amber glow that spreads across the sky. The sea sighs against the cliffs, sending frothy, silver‑kissed waves leaping over dark ledges before retreating into a deep, bruised‑purple horizon. Perched defiantly atop the highest crag, the lighthouse flickers its first steady beam, a solitary eye cutting through the gathering gloom, its lantern casting a thin ribbon of gold that sweeps across the restless surf and paints the weathered stone with a fleeting, warm light.
