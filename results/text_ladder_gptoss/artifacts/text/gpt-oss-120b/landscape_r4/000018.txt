The sun sinks behind the jagged cliffs, casting a bruised‑purple glow across the restless sea. Rough, weather‑worn rocks spill down to the surf, their edges glinting silver as the tide pulls back in frothy white ribbons. At the tip of the headland, the lighthouse stands like a steadfast sentinel, its white stone tower catching the last amber rays before the lantern awakens, a steady beam cutting through the growing twilight and guiding ships home through the amber‑tinged mist that rolls off the water.
