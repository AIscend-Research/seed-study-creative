As the sun slips below the horizon, the jagged headland is bathed in a subdued, amber‑gold glow that kisses the weather‑worn cliffs and the frothy surf that drapes its base. Dark, twisted rocks jut out like ancient sentinels, their surfaces slick with sea spray, while gulls wheel overhead, their silhouettes stark against the bruised‑purple sky. At the very tip, the lighthouse stands resolute—a stout, white tower crowned with a rust‑red lantern, its glass panes catching the last light and beginning to pulse a steady, amber warning. The sea sighs against the shore, and the air carries the salty tang of tide mingled with the faint, comforting scent of oil from the lighthouse’s lamp, heralding the night’s arrival over the restless coast.
