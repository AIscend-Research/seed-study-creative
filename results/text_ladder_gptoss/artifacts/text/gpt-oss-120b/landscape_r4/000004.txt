As the sun sinks behind the jagged cliffs, the sky deepens to a bruised violet, and the sea catches the last molten streaks of amber before turning a restless, steel‑blue. Weather‑worn rocks jut out like pale, ancient sentinels, their surfaces slick with spray that glitters in the fading light. At the headland’s tip, the lighthouse stands solitary and stoic, its white tower edged in rust‑red, the lantern room flickering to life as a warm, steady beam begins to sweep across the darkening waves, a guardian’s eye watching over the restless tide.
